What SkyFire is

SkyFire shows forecasts for sunrise and sunset colour, aurora viewing, Denali visibility and related sky conditions in South-central Alaska, made from public weather models. Forecasts are a planning guide, not a guarantee: the sky may do something different. Never rely on SkyFire for safety decisions (travel, weather hazards, avalanche terrain, sea or ice conditions).
