SkyFire privacy

Draft for review. Last updated November 2026.

What stays on your device

Your settings (tab, units, pin location, panel size) and, with SkyFire Pro, your licence key are stored only on your phone or computer. SkyFire has no user accounts and no tracking or advertising.

What is sent elsewhere

  • To show forecasts for a spot, the app asks for weather and elevation at points around it, from Open-Meteo directly or, with Pro, through the SkyFire Pro server. These requests carry map coordinates, not who you are.
  • Live space weather comes from NOAA, night-light imagery from NASA, and map tiles from the map provider.

SkyFire Pro purchases

Payments are handled by Stripe; SkyFire never sees your card. The SkyFire Pro server keeps your email, your Stripe customer and subscription IDs and the subscription status, to check your licence. It doesn't sell or share them. Ask through the contact page to have them deleted after you cancel.
